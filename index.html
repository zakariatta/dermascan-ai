How the AI Model Works

Our system utilizes a state-of-the-art convolutional neural network trained on extensive, annotated dermatological image datasets. The model is designed to identify visual features commonly associated with melanoma and other skin cancers.

It outputs a probability score based on pattern recognition — it is not a diagnosis. The model's accuracy is highly dependent on image quality, focus, and lighting conditions.

Regular dermatological checkups remain essential regardless of AI output.
